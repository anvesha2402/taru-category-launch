# TARU brand voice (condensed from the brand book, section 2)

TARU speaks like a respected elder who has checked the facts: few words, specific, warm without flattery. It proves before it persuades.

## Personality: each trait and the line it must not cross
- Assured, never boastful: state facts and let them stand; no superlatives.
- Warm, never sentimental: talk about the occasion and the person, not feelings in the abstract.
- Precise, never technical: give the number and say what it means.
- Rooted, never nostalgic: heritage is lived today; no "like our grandfathers wore".
- Understated, never cold: quiet, but every message ends with something useful to the reader.

## Tone by context
- Social post: conversational, curious, still exact. Example: "What does 'certified organic' actually certify? One post, four steps, no jargon."
- Product page: informative, sensory, specific. Example: "Mid-weight linen with a soft finish. Cut 4 cm fuller through the waist than our standard block, with extra seam allowance for alterations."
- Wedding-season and occasion: warm, occasion-first. Example: "For the day you stand next to your son."
- Gift messaging: considerate, understated. Example: "Chosen with care. The proof is inside the box, if he wants it."
- Email: plain and useful; one idea per paragraph; one clear next step.

## Rules
- Short sentences, one idea each; under 25 words in body copy.
- No exclamation marks. No emojis. No hashtags in body copy.
- Numerals and units: "₹4,999", "30 days", "95%". Indian English spelling (colour, centre, licence).
- Name what a certificate covers ("the fabric"), never blur product and packaging.
- Fit is respect: no body shaming ("hides the belly", "slimming").
- Talk about his occasions. Address men by role (fathers, elders), never "old" or "senior citizens".

## Word bank
- Use: certified, licence, trail, grown, woven, cut, fitted, altered, occasion, handed down, proof, check, see for yourself, alteration, repair.
- Avoid: eco, green, sustainable, natural, conscious, guilt-free, clean, pure, luxury, premium, exclusive, curated, timeless, iconic, heritage-inspired, transparent, honest (about ourselves), perfect.
- Comfort words (comfortable, soft, breathable) only with an approved claim; none is approved yet, so do not use them.

## Never
Testimonials or reviews, invented customers, fake scarcity ("only a few left", "hurry"), celebrity references, competitor names, and any claim not in the approved list.
