# White-space check (Step 0.3)

**Checked:** 29 September 2026 · **Question:** Does any Indian brand now sell certified-organic men's ethnic wear?

## Verdict

**The white space still holds, but it is narrower than the brief says.** No brand was found positioning itself on *certified-organic men's occasion or festive ethnic wear* (the Festive and Ceremonial lines). "Organic" men's kurtas do exist at the basic, everyday end, so TARU cannot say it is the "first" or "only" organic menswear brand.

## What was found

| Player | What they sell | Organic claim as shown | Relevance to TARU |
|---|---|---|---|
| Isha Life | Men's undyed organic cotton kurta, about ₹750 | Page says "pure organic cotton" and "authentic organic cotton"; no certifier, licence number or % shown | A direct precedent for an *everyday* organic men's kurta at a low price point. Kills any "first organic men's kurta" claim. |
| Bhusattva | Designer ethnic wear sold via Pernia's Pop-Up Shop and Aza | Markets itself as certified organic and lists certifications on its site | Certified-organic ethnic wear exists at the designer end. The listings found are women's; no men's line was confirmed. |
| Eco Clothing India | Kurtas (women's) and men's shirts; also does private label | "Internationally-certified organic fabrics", no specific certification shown | Adjacent. Could also be a manufacturing lead for the cost stack. |
| Fabindia | Large men's kurta range | No certified-organic menswear line found (per the brief) | Nearest mainstream frame of reference for "natural fabric" menswear |
| Yoga Shoppi (Bangladesh) | Men's 100% organic cotton kurta, "GOTS Certified", handspun | GOTS claimed | Out of market, but shows the product can be made and certified |

## Implications

1. **Positioning claim.** Frame the white space as *certified-organic men's occasion wear for mature builds*, not "organic menswear". Any comparative or "first" claim needs its own row in the claims matrix (D7) and is likely unsubstantiable.
2. **Price anchor.** Isha Life's ~₹750 organic kurta sets a low anchor for "organic" alone. The premium has to come from occasion, fit and proof, not the word "organic". Add Isha Life to the price audit.
3. **Scorecard.** Add Isha Life and Bhusattva to the competitor scorecard as adjacent players.
4. **Recheck.** Repeat this check before the launch deck is finalised.
